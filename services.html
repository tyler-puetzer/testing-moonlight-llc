SAFETY AT HOME

Fall Prevention

Practical support to help reduce common fall risks and make everyday movement around the home safer.

  • Identify common trip and fall hazards
  • Keep walkways and frequently used areas clear
  • Support safer routines around the home
  • Encourage use of recommended safety equipment

Fall prevention support is not a substitute for a medical evaluation or physical therapy. We can work alongside your healthcare team’s recommendations.
