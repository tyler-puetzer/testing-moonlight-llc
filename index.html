THE HEART BEHIND THE CARE

Meet the founders

Divine Moonlight was built from a desire to make home care feel personal. Behind the company are people who believe that caring for a loved one means showing up with patience, respect, compassion, and professionalism.

Co-Founder Tobby Allen
01 CO-FOUNDER · CARE & SERVICE

A background built around serving others.

Tobby brings experience as a former Army medic and licensed practical nurse to Divine Moonlight. His background in medical care has included working with individuals facing serious and complex care needs, including stroke and memory-care situations.

That experience shapes the way he approaches home care: with patience, dignity, compassion, and a genuine commitment to the people and families being served.

“Crowning your golden years” means treating every person with the respect they deserve.

02 CO-FOUNDER · VISION & FAMILY
